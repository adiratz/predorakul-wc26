Where this started

PredOrakul began as a World Cup prediction league between a small group of friends — nine predictors, 104 matches, way too much banter about who actually knew football and who was just guessing.

That version is still live, and it's the reason this one exists at all.

Now, for every Premier League weekend

The World Cup only comes around once every four years. Football doesn't. So PredOrakul is back for the 2026/27 Premier League season — same obsession with getting predictions exactly right, but built out properly this time, with a deeper scoring system and a genuine strategic layer we never had in the World Cup version.

What's different this time

Every scoreline you predict can score in five different ways at once — get the winner right, the exact score, the margin, an upset, or nail everything simultaneously for the biggest bonus in the game. It's not one prediction, one outcome — it's five chances to be right, stacked on top of each other.

The Dugout
Five tactical cards, one per fixture — from playing it safe to going all-in on a result you're sure about.
Upset Bonus
Automatic points for correctly calling a genuine underdog win — no card needed, just a sharp eye.
Champion Pick
One pick before Gameweek 1 — who lifts the title in May? Locked in early, resolved at the very end.
Shadow Table Coming soon
Your predictions quietly build a full personalized league table all season — a byproduct of picks you're already making.

Same spirit, sharper game

It's still built for banter first, bragging rights second. Just with a scoring system that finally rewards reading a match properly — not just picking a winner and hoping.
