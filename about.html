The Idea

PredOrakul started as a WhatsApp argument. A couple of friends, all convinced they read the game better than the others, needed a way to settle it. Not just predicting winners — anyone can do that. Scorelines. Goalscorers. Extra time. Penalties. Every match, every detail, for the entire World Cup.

The name comes from Predict and Oracle — the person who sees what others don't. There is exactly one PredOrakul per tournament. The rest are just guessing.

How It Works

Before each match kicks off, players submit their full prediction — who wins, what the score is, and who scores. Once the whistle blows, predictions are locked. No changes, no excuses. Points are awarded after the result, and the leaderboard updates in real time.

Knockout games go deeper. If you think it goes to extra time, predict that too. Penalties? Pick the shootout score. The further you go, the more you can earn — but also the more you can lose.

Perfect Prediction Bonus

Get everything right — outcome, scoreline, every goalscorer — and you earn a Perfect Prediction bonus on top of your regular points. In knockout games, going deeper earns a higher tier.
