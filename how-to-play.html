How to Play

Three steps, every matchweek. Read this once and you won't need to think about the mechanics again.

1

Enter a scoreline for each match

That's genuinely all you do: type in a score, like 2-1. You don't separately pick a winner, or say how big the margin is, or call a draw. Everything else is worked out automatically from the score you enter.

2

Play a Dugout Card, if you want one

Before a match locks, you can optionally play one of five cards to boost your points on that fixture. Totally optional, see below for what each one does.

3

Submit before the deadline

Predictions lock 1 hour before kickoff. You can change your mind and update a prediction as many times as you like right up until then.

How your scoreline earns points

One scoreline can score in several different ways at once. You're not choosing between them, they all stack:

  • Get the winner right → points
  • Get the exact score right → more points
  • Get the margin right, even if the exact score is off → more points
  • Correctly call an upset → automatic bonus
  • Nail everything at once → biggest bonus of all

You don't need to think about any of this while predicting, just enter the score you genuinely believe. The scoring engine does the rest.
